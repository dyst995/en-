## Words

### hello
**Definition:** A friendly greeting used when you meet someone.
**Example:** Hello, I’m Nika. It’s nice to meet you.
**IPA:** /həˈloʊ/

### hi
**Definition:** An informal way to say hello.
**Example:** Hi, are you free for coffee later?
**IPA:** /haɪ/
**Notes:** Use with friends, classmates, and coworkers you know well.

### goodbye
**Definition:** What you say when you leave or end a conversation.
**Example:** Goodbye, and thanks for your help today.
**IPA:** /ˌɡʊdˈbaɪ/

### thanks
**Definition:** A short word for showing you are grateful.
**Example:** Thanks for waiting for me.
**IPA:** /θæŋks/

### please
**Definition:** A polite word you add when you ask for something.
**Example:** Could I have some water, please?
**IPA:** /pliːz/

### welcome
**Definition:** What you say to greet someone who has arrived, or as a reply to “thank you.”
**Example:** Welcome to the class. Please take a seat.
**IPA:** /ˈwelkəm/
**Notes:** “You’re welcome” is the usual reply to “thank you.”

## Phrases

### How are you?
**Definition:** A polite question about how someone is doing.
**Example:** Hi Sara, how are you?
**Notes:** A common short reply is “I’m good, thanks. And you?”

### Nice to meet you
**Definition:** What you say when you meet someone for the first time.
**Example:** I’m Alex. Nice to meet you.
**Notes:** After the first meeting, say “Nice to see you.”

### What’s your name?
**Definition:** A direct question used to learn someone’s name.
**Example:** Hi, I don’t think we’ve met. What’s your name?

### Have a good day
**Definition:** A friendly way to end a conversation and wish someone well.
**Example:** It was good talking to you. Have a good day.

### See you later
**Definition:** An informal goodbye when you expect to meet again.
**Example:** I have to run, but see you later.
**Notes:** “See you soon” and “Catch you later” are close alternatives.

### I’m fine, thanks
**Definition:** A common, polite reply to “How are you?”
**Example:** “How are you?” — “I’m fine, thanks. How about you?”
