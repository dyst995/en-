## Words

### always
**Definition:** Every time; without exception.
**Example:** I always drink water when I wake up.
**IPA:** /ˈɔːlweɪz/

### usually
**Definition:** Most of the time, but not always.
**Example:** I usually finish work around six.
**IPA:** /ˈjuːʒuəli/

### sometimes
**Definition:** On some occasions, but not often or regularly.
**Example:** Sometimes I walk home instead of taking the bus.
**IPA:** /ˈsʌmtaɪmz/

### never
**Definition:** Not at any time; not once.
**Example:** I never skip breakfast if I can help it.
**IPA:** /ˈnevər/

### busy
**Definition:** Having a lot to do; not free.
**Example:** This week is busy, so I can’t meet on Wednesday.
**IPA:** /ˈbɪzi/

### late
**Definition:** After the expected or planned time.
**Example:** The train was late, so I missed the start of class.
**IPA:** /leɪt/

### routine
**Definition:** The usual order of things you do each day.
**Example:** Exercise is part of my morning routine.
**IPA:** /ruːˈtiːn/

## Phrases

### What do you do?
**Definition:** A common question about someone’s job or studies.
**Example:** What do you do? I teach English at a language school.
**Notes:** People sometimes answer with both work and a personal detail.

### I wake up at…
**Definition:** A way to say what time your day starts.
**Example:** I wake up at seven and leave the house by eight.

### I’m running late
**Definition:** You will arrive after the time you planned.
**Example:** I’m running late — can we start ten minutes later?
**Notes:** Useful in messages before a meeting or class.

### Let’s catch up
**Definition:** A friendly suggestion to talk and share news.
**Example:** It’s been a while. Let’s catch up this weekend.

### How’s your day going?
**Definition:** An informal way to ask about someone’s day so far.
**Example:** Hey, how’s your day going? Need a coffee break?

### I’ll get back to you
**Definition:** A promise to reply later, usually after you check something.
**Example:** I don’t have the answer yet, but I’ll get back to you this afternoon.
