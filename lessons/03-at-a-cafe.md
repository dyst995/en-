## Words

### order
**Definition:** To ask for food or drink in a café or restaurant; also the request itself.
**Example:** I’d like to order a cappuccino and a croissant.
**IPA:** /ˈɔːrdər/

### menu
**Definition:** The list of food and drinks you can buy.
**Example:** Could I see the menu, please?
**IPA:** /ˈmenjuː/

### bill
**Definition:** The piece of paper that shows how much you need to pay.
**Example:** Could we have the bill when you have a moment?
**IPA:** /bɪl/
**Notes:** In American English, people often say “check.”

### takeaway
**Definition:** Food or drink you take with you instead of eating in.
**Example:** One tea to takeaway, please.
**IPA:** /ˈteɪkəweɪ/
**Notes:** American English: “to go.”

### latte
**Definition:** A coffee drink made with espresso and steamed milk.
**Example:** I’ll have a small latte, please.
**IPA:** /ˈlɑːteɪ/

### refill
**Definition:** Another serving of the same drink.
**Example:** Could I get a refill of water?
**IPA:** /ˈriːfɪl/

### recommend
**Definition:** To suggest something you think is good.
**Example:** What would you recommend for a cold morning?
**IPA:** /ˌrekəˈmend/

## Phrases

### Can I get…?
**Definition:** A casual, natural way to order.
**Example:** Can I get an iced latte and a banana bread, please?

### For here or to go?
**Definition:** A staff question: will you stay, or take the order with you?
**Example:** “For here or to go?” — “For here, please.”

### Could I have the bill, please?
**Definition:** A polite way to ask to pay.
**Example:** That was lovely. Could I have the bill, please?

### I’m just looking
**Definition:** A polite way to say you do not want help yet.
**Example:** “Can I help you?” — “I’m just looking, thanks.”

### Does this come with…?
**Definition:** A question about what is included with a dish or drink.
**Example:** Does this come with milk, or is that extra?

### I’ll have the same
**Definition:** A short way to order what someone else just ordered.
**Example:** That sounds perfect. I’ll have the same.
